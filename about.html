ABOUT FAM

Architects, engineers and logistics experts, working as one company.

We are a group of Architects, Structural Engineers, Project Managers and Logistics experts. We build homes that live up to their names. Each project is a masterpiece of design and construction, backed by comprehensive quality assurance from first sketch to final handover.

We are founded on four principles — Service, Integrity, Professionalism and Accountability — and committed to exceeding client expectations and building long-term relationships.

FAM has successfully executed jobs for both private individuals and corporate bodies — from residential estates to public-sector renovation and industrial design work.
