Who we are

We build homes that live up to their names. Each project is a masterpiece of design and construction, backed by comprehensive quality assurance from first sketch to final handover.

We are a group of Architects, Structural Engineers, Project Managers and Logistics experts, founded on four principles: Service, Integrity, Professionalism and Accountability. FAM has executed jobs for private individuals and corporate bodies alike, from residential estates to public-sector and military developments and industrial design work.
